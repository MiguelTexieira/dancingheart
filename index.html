About

A bit about me

I first found yoga more than ten years ago while looking for a way to stay active and manage stress. My first teacher was Kirsty Brooks, and what began as a physical practice quickly became something much deeper – a space for reflection, strength, and growth. Today I'm proud to cover classes for Kirsty myself. In 2013, I completed my 200-hour yoga teacher training with Himalaya Yoga Valley Centre in Goa, India. Their training, rooted in classical Hatha and Ashtanga yoga, gave me a strong foundation in alignment, breathwork, and yogic philosophy.

Over the years, I’ve continued to study, grow, and share what I learn with others. Teaching Vinyasa Flow is my passion – I love how each practice can be both energising and grounding. My goal is to create classes that are welcoming, inclusive, and adaptable. I believe yoga should meet you where you are, and I’ll always offer options and support to help you feel confident and at ease.

Classes

Private One-to-One Sessions

If you’d prefer a more personal approach, I offer private yoga sessions tailored to your individual needs and goals. These are ideal if you're just starting out, working with an injury, or wanting to deepen your practice with focused attention. I’ll design each session just for you – whether you’re looking to build strength, improve flexibility, or find a moment of calm in your busy week.

Small Group Classes

Gather a few friends, family, neighbours or colleagues and I’ll come to you. I teach small group classes at your home, garden or chosen space, so you can practise together somewhere comfortable and familiar. Classes are welcoming, energising and suitable for all levels – we move mindfully through breath-led sequences, building strength while cultivating calm. Whether it’s your first class or your hundredth, you’ll be supported and encouraged to move at your own pace.

Specialized Classes

I also offer specialized yoga sessions designed for specific groups and needs. Each session is thoughtfully adapted to create a safe, enjoyable, and beneficial practice.

Family Yoga

Bring the whole family together for a fun, playful yoga session. These classes are designed to be enjoyable for all ages, combining movement, breathing, and relaxation in a way that everyone can participate in and enjoy together.

Couples Yoga

Deepen your connection through partnered yoga. These sessions help couples build trust, communication, and harmony through supported poses and synchronized breathing. Perfect for all levels and a wonderful way to share the practice together.

Chair Yoga

Gentle, accessible yoga practiced with the support of a chair. Ideal for those with limited mobility, balance concerns, or anyone seeking a softer approach. Build strength, improve flexibility, and enjoy the benefits of yoga in a comfortable, supportive way.
